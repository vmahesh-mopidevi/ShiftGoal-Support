ShiftGoal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 July 2026

ShiftGoal is designed with privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ShiftGoal iOS application.

Information You Enter

ShiftGoal allows you to enter information such as workplaces, working hours, hourly rates, payments, financial goals, expenses, notes, tips and bonuses.

How Information Is Stored

ShiftGoal stores app information locally on your device using Apple's storage technologies. ShiftGoal does not require you to create an account.

Information Collection

ShiftGoal does not collect, sell or use your personal information for advertising. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Face ID

If you enable app protection, authentication is performed using Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. ShiftGoal does not receive or store your biometric information.

Notifications

ShiftGoal may request permission to send reminders. Notifications are optional and can be disabled through the app or iOS Settings.

Home Screen Widgets

ShiftGoal may share a limited app summary with its Home Screen widget through an Apple App Group stored on your device.

Data Export

You may choose to export your information as CSV files. Exported data only leaves the app when you intentionally save or share it using Apple's share sheet.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains on your device until you remove records from the app or uninstall ShiftGoal, subject to Apple's backup and restore behaviour.

Children's Privacy

ShiftGoal is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ShiftGoal's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

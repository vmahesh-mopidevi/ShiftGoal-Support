ShiftGoal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

ShiftGoal respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ShiftGoal iOS application.

Privacy at a glance

ShiftGoal is designed as a local-first work-hours, earnings and payment tracking application.

Your work records are stored on your device and may synchronize through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available.

ShiftGoal does not operate its own server for storing your workplaces, shifts, earnings, payments, expenses or goals.

ShiftGoal uses TelemetryDeck for limited product analytics. Your detailed work and financial records are not intentionally sent to TelemetryDeck.

1. Information you enter into ShiftGoal

ShiftGoal helps you track working hours, expected earnings, payments, expenses, goals and related work information.

Depending on how you use ShiftGoal, you may enter information including:

  • workplace names
  • work dates and shift times
  • hours worked
  • break durations
  • hourly rates
  • overtime rates and overtime hours
  • tips and bonuses
  • expected earnings
  • payment amounts
  • gross and net pay
  • tax, National Insurance, pension and other deduction amounts
  • income and hours goals
  • work-related expenses
  • days off and other work-day statuses
  • pay schedules
  • saved shift templates
  • notes you choose to enter
  • workplace settings
  • app preferences
  • an optional display name used for personalisation
  • milestone acknowledgement information
  • weekly goal celebration acknowledgement information

ShiftGoal does not require a separate ShiftGoal account.

You are not required to provide ShiftGoal with an email address, phone number, password or payment-card information in order to use the app.

2. Local storage

ShiftGoal stores your records locally on your device using Apple's application storage technologies, including SwiftData.

Application preferences may also be stored locally using Apple-provided storage, including settings relating to:

  • currency
  • week-start preferences
  • appearance
  • notifications
  • Face ID or device authentication
  • onboarding
  • display name
  • reminder settings
  • What's New status
  • iCloud-related app state

The ShiftGoal widget uses an Apple App Group so that a limited summary required by the widget can be shared locally between the ShiftGoal app and its own widget extension.

3. iCloud and CloudKit

When you are signed into iCloud and iCloud services are available, ShiftGoal may synchronize supported records using Apple's CloudKit service and your private iCloud database.

This can allow your ShiftGoal information to synchronize or restore between devices associated with the same iCloud account.

Information that may synchronize through your private iCloud database includes:

  • workplaces
  • shifts
  • payments
  • income and hours goals
  • work-related expenses
  • work-day statuses
  • saved shift templates
  • profile and personalisation information used by ShiftGoal
  • milestone acknowledgements
  • weekly goal celebration acknowledgements

ShiftGoal uses a private CloudKit database for this synchronization.

ShiftGoal does not operate its own developer-controlled server for storing your workplaces, shifts, payments, earnings, expenses or goals.

If iCloud is unavailable, ShiftGoal may continue operating using local storage, although synchronization and cloud restoration may not be available.

4. iCloud connection verification

ShiftGoal includes a feature called Verify iCloud Connection.

When you choose to run this check, ShiftGoal may temporarily create a small diagnostic record in your private iCloud database.

The diagnostic record may contain technical information such as:

  • the time of the connection check
  • an identifier showing that the record was created by ShiftGoal

The diagnostic record does not contain your workplaces, shifts, payment amounts, earnings, expenses or notes.

It is used to verify the iCloud connection and is then deleted.

5. Analytics

ShiftGoal uses TelemetryDeck to understand how features are used and to help improve the app.

ShiftGoal sends limited product-interaction events when certain features are used.

These events may include simple information such as:

  • whether a newly created shift was entered manually, repeated from the previous shift, or created from a saved template
  • whether Week, Month or Year was selected in Insights
  • the installed ShiftGoal version when What's New is opened for a new version
  • interactions such as creating, editing or deleting certain records, opening Calendar or Insights, using Quick Fill, or using saved templates

TelemetryDeck also attaches technical information used for analytics, including information such as:

  • ShiftGoal app version and build number
  • iOS version
  • device model
  • language
  • locale and region
  • time zone
  • screen size and scale
  • platform information
  • session information
  • session-usage statistics
  • TelemetryDeck SDK information
  • a pseudonymous device identifier used for analytics

On iOS, TelemetryDeck uses a hashed form of Apple's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) for analytics.

ShiftGoal does not provide TelemetryDeck with your name, email address, iCloud account information, CloudKit user identifier or a separate ShiftGoal account identifier.

ShiftGoal does not intentionally send the following information to TelemetryDeck:

  • workplace names
  • workplace identifiers
  • shift dates
  • exact start or finish times
  • hours worked
  • break durations
  • hourly rates
  • overtime rates
  • tips or bonuses
  • expected earnings
  • payment amounts
  • gross or net pay
  • tax, pension or deduction amounts
  • expense amounts
  • expense categories
  • goal amounts
  • saved shift-template names
  • your optional display name
  • notes
  • other free-text work information
  • device location
  • notification content

Analytics are enabled by default in the current version of ShiftGoal.

ShiftGoal does not currently provide an in-app control for disabling TelemetryDeck analytics.

TelemetryDeck is used for product analytics only.

ShiftGoal does not use TelemetryDeck for advertising or cross-app tracking.

ShiftGoal does not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising and tracking

ShiftGoal does not display third-party advertising.

ShiftGoal does not use your information for behavioural advertising, targeted advertising or cross-app tracking.

ShiftGoal does not use Apple's IDFA for advertising.

ShiftGoal does not currently request App Tracking Transparency permission because ShiftGoal does not use your data for cross-app tracking.

7. Face ID and device authentication

ShiftGoal may allow you to protect access to the app using Face ID, Optic ID or another authentication method supported by your device.

Authentication is handled by Apple's operating-system security technologies.

ShiftGoal does not receive or store your Face ID, Optic ID or other biometric template data.

ShiftGoal receives only the result of the authentication request, such as whether authentication succeeded or failed.

8. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, ShiftGoal may schedule local notifications relating to features such as:

  • shift reminders
  • missing-shift reminders
  • goal reminders or check-ins
  • payday reminders
  • weekly reviews

For example, if you enable the Missing Shift Reminder, ShiftGoal may notify you when the previous day does not contain a logged shift and has not been marked as a day off.

ShiftGoal schedules its app reminders locally on your device.

ShiftGoal does not operate its own remote marketing-notification server.

Apple services, including CloudKit, may use system notifications in the background to support synchronization.

You can manage ShiftGoal notification permissions through iOS Settings.

9. CSV exports and sharing

ShiftGoal allows you to export certain information as CSV files.

Exports are initiated by you.

Exported files are created locally before the system sharing interface is presented.

When you choose to save or share an exported file, the destination is selected using Apple's sharing interface.

You may choose destinations such as Files, Mail, AirDrop or another compatible application installed on your device.

ShiftGoal does not automatically upload your CSV exports to a ShiftGoal server.

Once you choose to send information to another application or service, that application's own privacy practices apply.

10. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted through ShiftGoal, removed as part of normal app operation, or the app's local storage is removed.

Where supported by the app, individual records such as shifts, payments, expenses, goals, workplaces and saved shift templates may be edited or deleted from within ShiftGoal.

Deleting ShiftGoal from your device normally removes its local application data from that device.

If ShiftGoal information has previously synchronized with your private iCloud account, deleting the app does not necessarily remove the copy stored in iCloud.

This means synchronized information may become available again after reinstalling ShiftGoal while using the same iCloud account.

You can manage iCloud data using controls provided by Apple through your device and Apple account.

Because ShiftGoal does not operate a separate ShiftGoal account system, there is no separate ShiftGoal account that needs to be deleted.

Analytics information sent to TelemetryDeck is subject to TelemetryDeck's own retention and privacy practices.

11. Security

ShiftGoal relies on security protections provided by iOS and, when iCloud synchronization is used, Apple's iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure.

Optional device authentication can provide an additional device-level access control for ShiftGoal.

No electronic storage or synchronization system can guarantee absolute security.

ShiftGoal does not claim that application data is independently end-to-end encrypted by ShiftGoal itself.

12. Third-party and platform services

ShiftGoal currently uses services including:

  • Apple iCloud and CloudKit for supported synchronization of ShiftGoal records through the user's private iCloud database.
  • TelemetryDeck for privacy-focused product analytics. TelemetryDeck receives limited product-interaction and technical information as described in the Analytics section above.

Information processed by these services is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

External links

ShiftGoal may include links to external websites or services, such as the ShiftGoal support website, GitHub or LinkedIn.

These links are opened only when you choose to use them.

Once you leave ShiftGoal, information handled by those external websites or services is subject to their own privacy policies and practices.

13. Children's privacy

ShiftGoal is a general-purpose work-hours, pay and earnings tracking application and is not specifically directed toward children.

ShiftGoal does not operate third-party advertising or a separate ShiftGoal user-account system designed to collect children's personal information.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ShiftGoal's features, data practices, third-party services or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the Last updated date at the top of this page will be revised.

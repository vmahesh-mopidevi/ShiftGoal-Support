ShiftGoal Support

Help and support for the ShiftGoal iOS application.

About ShiftGoal

ShiftGoal helps hourly workers log shifts, understand expected earnings, compare recorded payments, track work-related expenses and monitor income or hours goals.

ShiftGoal is designed to give you a clearer picture of the hours you have worked, what you expect to earn, what you have actually received and how your work is progressing over time.

ShiftGoal does not require a separate account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my information stored?

ShiftGoal stores your workplaces, shifts, payments, goals, expenses, work-day statuses, saved shift templates and related information locally on your device using Apple technologies.

When you are signed into iCloud and iCloud services are available, supported ShiftGoal records may also synchronize using Apple's CloudKit service and your private iCloud database.

This can allow your information to synchronize or restore between devices using the same iCloud account.

ShiftGoal does not operate its own server for storing your work and financial records.

Does ShiftGoal sync with iCloud?

Yes. ShiftGoal can use your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit service when iCloud is available.

If iCloud is unavailable, ShiftGoal can continue using local storage, although synchronization and cloud restoration may not be available.

How are my working hours calculated?

ShiftGoal supports both time-based shift entry and Total Hours entry.

For time-based shifts, ShiftGoal calculates your worked time using the start time, finish time and break information you enter.

For Total Hours shifts, the number of hours entered by you is used directly.

How are expected earnings calculated?

Expected earnings are estimated from information you enter into ShiftGoal, including your working hours, hourly rate and, where applicable, overtime, tips and bonuses.

ShiftGoal is a personal tracking and estimation tool. It is not an official payroll system and does not replace your employer's payslip.

What is Repeat Last Shift?

Repeat Last Shift helps you quickly fill in a new shift using the pattern from your most recent eligible shift.

ShiftGoal fills the form for you, but the shift is not automatically saved. You can review and change the details before saving it.

What are Shift Templates?

Shift Templates let you save working patterns that you use regularly.

For example, you could save a Morning Shift or Evening Shift template and select it from Quick Fill when logging a new shift.

Applying a template fills the shift form. You can still review and edit the information before saving.

How do pay schedules work?

A workplace can have its own pay schedule.

Depending on the schedule you configure, ShiftGoal can work with weekly, every-two-weeks or monthly pay periods.

ShiftGoal uses the configured schedule to associate a scheduled payday with the relevant working period.

Why does a payment show as recorded even when the amount is different?

A payment is considered recorded when you have entered a matching payment for that pay period.

ShiftGoal separately shows whether the amount received was equal to, lower than or higher than the expected amount.

For example, if you expected £343 and recorded £300, ShiftGoal can show that the payment has been recorded while also showing that you received £43 less than expected.

How do Insights work?

Insights helps you understand your working patterns and financial information across Week, Month and Year views.

Depending on your data, Insights can show information such as:

  • hours worked
  • expected earnings
  • expenses
  • projected net earnings
  • workplace breakdowns
  • working patterns
  • goal progress
  • scheduled payday information
  • expected versus recorded payments

Payment information in Insights follows the pay schedule associated with the workplace rather than simply treating every calendar week as a pay period.

What is Projected Net?

Projected Net is based on the expected earnings and work-related expenses within the selected Insights period.

It is an estimate and should not be treated as an official payroll, tax or accounting figure.

What is the Missing Shift Reminder?

The Missing Shift Reminder is an optional notification that can help you remember to log a day.

When enabled, ShiftGoal can remind you if the previous day does not contain a logged shift and has not been marked as a day off.

The reminder uses local notifications and can be disabled through ShiftGoal or your iPhone notification settings.

Can I use ShiftGoal for multiple workplaces?

Yes. You can create and manage multiple workplaces.

Each workplace can have its own information such as hourly rate and supported pay schedule.

Does ShiftGoal use analytics?

ShiftGoal uses TelemetryDeck for limited, privacy-focused product analytics.

Analytics help the developer understand which ShiftGoal features are being used and where the app can be improved.

ShiftGoal does not intentionally send your workplace names, detailed shift information, hourly rates, earnings, payments, expenses, goals, notes or other free-text work information to TelemetryDeck.

ShiftGoal does not use TelemetryDeck for advertising or cross-app tracking.

For full details, read the ShiftGoal Privacy Policy.

How does Face ID protection work?

If you enable device authentication, ShiftGoal can use security provided by iOS, such as Face ID or another supported authentication method.

Apple handles the biometric authentication. ShiftGoal does not receive or store your biometric template.

How do Home Screen widgets work?

ShiftGoal can display selected summary information through its Home Screen widget.

A limited snapshot required by the widget is shared locally between ShiftGoal and its own widget extension using an Apple App Group.

How do I export my information?

ShiftGoal supports CSV export for certain app records.

The export is created locally and presented through the iOS share sheet, allowing you to choose where to save or send it.

ShiftGoal does not automatically upload your CSV export to a ShiftGoal-controlled server.

How do I delete my information?

Individual ShiftGoal records can be edited or deleted within the app where that functionality is available.

Deleting ShiftGoal from your iPhone normally removes the locally stored application data from that device.

If information has previously synchronized with your private iCloud database, deleting the application does not necessarily delete the copy stored in iCloud.

That information may become available again if ShiftGoal is reinstalled while you are using the same iCloud account.

You can manage iCloud data using controls provided by Apple through your device and Apple account.

Report a Problem

If ShiftGoal is not working as expected, please contact support.

When reporting an issue, it is helpful to include:

  • your iPhone model
  • your iOS version
  • your ShiftGoal version
  • whether iCloud sync is available
  • the steps that caused the problem
  • what you expected to happen
  • what actually happened
  • a screenshot or screen recording where possible

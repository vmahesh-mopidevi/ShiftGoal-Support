ShiftGoal Support

Help and support for the ShiftGoal iOS application.

About ShiftGoal

ShiftGoal helps hourly-paid workers track shifts, calculate expected earnings, compare payments, manage work-related expenses and monitor income goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my information stored?

ShiftGoal stores your workplaces, shifts, payments, goals and expenses locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. ShiftGoal does not require an account.

How are earnings calculated?

Earnings are estimated using the hours, hourly rate, breaks, overtime, tips and bonuses entered by you. ShiftGoal is a personal tracking tool and is not an official payroll system.

Can I use ShiftGoal for multiple workplaces?

Yes. You can create multiple workplaces with different hourly rates, overtime rates and payment records.

How do I protect my information?

You can enable Face ID protection from the app's Settings screen. Authentication is handled by Apple's operating system.

How do I export my information?

Open ShiftGoal and go to Settings, then Data & Privacy. You can export your records as CSV files and save or share them using the iOS share sheet.

How do I delete my information?

Individual records can be removed inside the app. Uninstalling ShiftGoal will remove locally stored app data from the device, subject to Apple's device backup and restore behaviour.
